● 05 How the network works · new

Every part of the network makes the others stronger

  2. Patients and families keep records, symptoms and questions organised, and bring them to every appointment.
  3. Hospitals coordinate care with less admin, and their clinics feed the network.
  4. The network builds a real-time registry and anonymised insights that help foundations, clinicians and researchers.

● 02 Outcomes · have
Icon / mini visual

Less admin, lighter workload

Better communication between teams and patients improves coordination of care. That means less administrative burden, lower mental workload for professionals, and a better experience for everyone.

Icon / mini visual

Achieve the Triple Aim

Reduce diagnostic delays, deliver better clinical care management, and improve quality of life for patients and their families.

Icon / mini visual

Lower cost of care

Better-coordinated care means fewer repeated tests, missed appointments and avoidable visits, which lowers costs for the healthcare system.
