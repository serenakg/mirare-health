Must have before launch

  1. Privacy policy, imprint, cookie notice

  4. Medical disclaimer (not a diagnosis tool)

  1. Founder story: why mirare exists
  2. Real quotes from parents and patients
  3. Product screens (even early mock-ups)
  4. "Who it's for" split: patients, parents, carers, clinicians

Builds trust with partners

  1. Advisory board / clinical partners / patient orgs logos
  2. Research page: how anonymised data is governed

● 03 About · have

Made for the people who carry complex care every day

mirare health was created for the people who carry complex care every day—patients, parents, carers, and families living with uncertainty, long waits for answers, and health concerns that are too often misunderstood or overlooked.

We are building a secure, patient-centred digital health solution for people affected by rare diseases, childhood cancer, and complex health conditions. A place to keep important health information together, make sense of what is happening, and feel more supported as you care for the people who need it the most.

Your health story can help shape better care and future treatments.

● 04 How it works · have
Screen: records + appointments
01

Keep everything in one place

Upload your health records, keep track of symptoms and appointments, and save the questions you want to ask. Your information stays organised and ready when you need it in your private space.

Screen: pattern / timeline view
02

Make sense of the patterns

We bring your health information together in one place, helping you, your family, and your care team build a clearer picture over time. Connect with relevant specialists worldwide and find support from a community of people who understand your experience.

Visual: many dots → one insight
03

Support the search for new treatments

If you choose to contribute your anonymised data, it is combined with information from many other people and analysed as group data. This helps reveal patterns that can improve understanding of rare diseases, support better care, and help researchers develop future treatments.

Unclear: step 02 packs in 3 features (patterns, specialists worldwide, community). Either split into its own "Features" row or cut to the core one for launch.
Check wording: "make sense of the patterns" can read as clinical interpretation. If the app does that, it may fall under EU medical device rules (MDR). Get a regulatory view on this line.
